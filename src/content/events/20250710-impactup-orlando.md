# ImpactUp community event - using data and technology to impact our community

## Details

Please register on [Luma](https://lu.ma/impactuporlando)!

Event hosted as part of the We Are For Good network's ImpactUp single day conversation hosted around the world focused on community impact.

We'll have community talks on using technology for impact, building connections, pivoting in work to create a new solution, and so much more. You'll hear from some of the most networked and most seasoned technologists in Central Florida talking about how they've built their careers and led with impact.

---

## This season’s theme: MOVEMENT

Because change doesn’t sit still.

At this meetup, we’ll explore:

- How to get unstuck in your mission
- How to move with purpose—not just speed
- How to grow a community that carries your work forward

Bring a friend. Bring your team. Bring your whole, authentic self. We’re building something bigger—one gathering at a time.

Want to go deeper? Start by joining the free virtual ImpactUp gathering on July 10 at [weareforgood.com/impactup](http://weareforgood.com/impactup)

Let’s get moving—together.
