# DevOps Storytime Social

## Details

Come join us again at The Gnarly Barley for a sammi and Beer or whatnot as we gather to share and listen to members stories. Every other month we will have story topics and welcome everyone to share their story. Stories are generally around 10 minutes in length with some Q&A after each one. Here is the schedule.

- March - Origin Stories
- May - Title (a)Bout (What I do at work)
- **July - Favorite DevOps Tool(s)**
- September - Aha! Moments (When something clicked.)
- November - Horror Stories (That time you broke the network, pushed bad code...)

There is no need for a slide deck, or computer. These are meant to be like just hanging around the bar, with your friends, telling a story... Because that is just exactly what it is.
